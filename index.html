03 / BEFORE YOU BUCKLE UP

A few good
questions.

Every confident driver starts with a question.

I’ve never driven before. Where do I start?+

The Beginner Driving course is the starting point. Enquire about an introductory lesson and discuss your experience before choosing a schedule.

Can I take lessons after a long break?+

Refresher lessons focus on rebuilding confidence and practising the skills you want to improve. Share what you would like to work on when you enquire.

How much do lessons cost?+

Course fees and lesson availability will be confirmed by the school. Ask for the latest package details before booking.

What should I bring to my first lesson?+

Ask the school which identification, learner permit, or other documents are required for your course and location before attending.
